4. Deletion Process

After receiving and verifying a valid request, we will identify the relevant personal data and delete it or anonymize it where appropriate, subject to applicable legal obligations.

We will communicate the outcome and any applicable retention exceptions.

5. Revoke Platform Access

Users may also revoke access through the connected platform's account settings, where supported.

Revoking access does not necessarily delete data previously stored by Trend Intelligence.

6. Third-Party Data

YAgen cannot directly delete data retained independently by TikTok, Meta, or other third-party platforms. Users should consult the relevant platform's privacy controls.
